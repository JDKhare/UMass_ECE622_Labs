B.5

Approach to find delta(S0) with k=17:

  • The SAT instance encodes the unrolled transition relation for k=17, starting from the fixed initial state at time 0.
  • A candidate state “s” is enforced by adding unit clauses that constrain the state variables at time 17 to equal s.
  • If the SAT solver returns SAT, there exists an input sequence of length 17 taking the machine from initial → s, hence s is reachable at transition
  • Repeat this for all 2^5 = 32 possible states; collect the ones that are SAT.

Following the steps as mentioned, we got the following 19 states to be reachable at k=17th unrolling of stoplight2.v:

{00000, 00001, 00010, 00011, 00100, 00101, 00110, 00111, 01000, 01001, 10000, 10001, 10010, 10011, 10100, 10101, 10110, 10111, 11000}
